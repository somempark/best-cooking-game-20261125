**Sizzle & Serve** — run a little burger stall through the lunch rush. Take orders from the board, grill patties before they burn, chop veg at the board, build each burger on the assembly plate, and serve it at the window before the customer's patience runs out. Built from scratch in vanilla JavaScript on a 2D canvas: no libraries, no network requests, no copied assets.

## What's in the game

- **A complete shift** — 150 seconds of lunch rush, up to four order tickets at once, each with its own patience bar, price and tip. Serve five orders to finish the shift; three missed or wrong orders ends it early.
- **Six dishes, one kitchen** — Classic, Cheese, Garden, Deluxe and Double Stack burgers plus Golden Fries, each a different combination of bun, patty, lettuce, tomato, cheese, sauce and fries.
- **Real cooking** — patties grill for five seconds and burn if you leave them, fries bubble in the fryer, lettuce and tomato must be chopped by holding E at the board. A burnt patty can only go in the bin.
- **Planning under pressure** — carry up to four items, plan a route between the crates, the grill, the board and the plate, and keep a combo going for bonus coins.
- **Feel and polish** — a sizzle loop while food cooks, chop ticks, a serve chime, floating coin popups, a red flash on a missed order, and an urgent pulse when a ticket is about to expire. **M** mutes.
- **Keyboard and mouse** — WASD or arrows to move, click to walk somewhere, **E** or **Space** to interact, **Enter** to open the stall and cook again.

## Run it

```sh
node serve.ts
```

Then open http://localhost:8080 in a desktop browser. Keyboard and mouse required.
